# Survey instrument

The questionnaire was administered at the end of the fall 2022 term. The nine item statements below reproduce the supplied response-workbook wording. Q1–Q9 are analysis labels, not original form item numbers.

## Evaluation and participation items

All nine statements used the same five-point agreement scale, from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). The intermediate verbal labels were not supplied; they are not reconstructed. Participation items are agreement statements, not frequency scales.

- **Q1:** I am satisfied with the interactive lab.
- **Q2:** Interactive lab sessions increased my interest for the course.
- **Q3:** The tools (VS Code Live Share and Microsoft Teams) used in the interactive lab sessions were useful.
- **Q4:** It was helpful that the lab materials were shared beforehand rather than during the lab.
- **Q5:** I shared my code for the review during the lab sessions.
- **Q6:** It was helpful to have my code reviewed by other students during the lab sessions.
- **Q7:** I reviewed other students' code during the lab sessions.
- **Q8:** Reviewing the other students' code improved my algorithmic thinking skills.
- **Q9:** I recommend interactive lab to be carried out in the next term.
